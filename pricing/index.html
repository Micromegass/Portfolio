Questions about price

Why is this cheaper than the agency down the road?

Because there is no agency around it. No account manager, no project manager, no office to pay for — the person you talk to is the person who builds your website. We also use AI seriously in our own workflow, which makes us faster. That shows up as a shorter timeline and a lower price, not as a worse website.

Is that really the final price?

Yes, for the scope we agree in writing before starting. The price only changes if you ask for something that was not in that scope — and then we tell you what it costs before we do it, never afterwards.

What if I do not know which one I need?

Send us your current website or just describe your business. We will tell you honestly which of these fits — including when the answer is that you do not need us at all.

Do you take payment in stages?

Half when we start, half when the site goes live. For larger projects we agree stages up front.

What does it cost to run afterwards?

The sites we build are static, which means hosting is a few euros a month rather than the monthly bill a heavy content management system needs. Our care plan on top is optional and starts at €39 a month.
